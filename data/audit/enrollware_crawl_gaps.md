# Enrollware crawl gaps and boundaries

The principal Admin menu tree, key detail screens, all Help categories and all visible notification history were covered. The following gaps are retained so later research does not overstate certainty.

## Unexplored or partially explored

- EnrollwarePay Portal dashboard/transaction/report/terminal/capital sub-navigation appeared as client-side `#` links. Entry was not forced or URL-guessed; Payment/Funding Report and Virtual Terminal surfaces were inspected instead.
- Subscription Billing was inventoried but no billing mutation/setup flow was entered.
- File downloads (XLSX/CSV/PDF), imports and exports were not executed. Their labels/formats were recorded from UI/help evidence.
- All 200 Help articles were inventoried, but only 68 high-value articles were opened and read in full. Remaining articles are predominantly basic setup, videos, billing and lower-priority how-to topics.
- Some announcement features are optional, edition-specific, retired or not enabled for this tenant. They are marked as historical operational evidence rather than current capability.
- Dashboard content was not treated as an authoritative workflow source; functional menus and reports were examined directly.
- No lower-privilege user session was available, so permission behavior was inferred from role/edit controls and help—not tested by impersonation.

## Mutation boundaries intentionally not crossed

- Create/edit/delete/archive course, class, participant, instructor, location, client, user, promo or product.
- Import, bulk delete, Quick Add/Add Student, move/reschedule, copy/repeat, waitlist promotion.
- Finalize/unlock roster, edit scores, issue/print/reconcile cards or certificates.
- Send/resend email/SMS, launch campaigns, upload documents.
- Process/void/refund/recharge payments, use virtual terminal, change QuickBooks sync state.
- Assign/recycle keycodes, change shipping/fulfillment status.
- Change settings, integrations, credentials, security or permissions.

## Authentication and evidence limitations

- Owner-authenticated administrator menus were available throughout; no authentication expiry occurred.
- No password, MFA code, cookie, token or integration secret was requested or captured.
- Screens containing participant data were inspected only as needed and were not screenshotted or reproduced in committed artifacts.
- Safe screenshots cover configuration/help structure only.
