American Heart Association

BLS Provider

Basic Life Support for healthcare professionals

BLS Provider teaches high-quality CPR, use of an AED, relief of choking, and effective team response for adults, children, and infants. It is designed for healthcare professionals and others whose work or training requires professional-level CPR certification.

This is not a First Aid or general lay-rescuer CPR class. Participants earn the AHA BLS Provider certification upon successful completion.
