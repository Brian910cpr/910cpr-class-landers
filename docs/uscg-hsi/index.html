Common questions

Before you register

Is HSINST-203 accepted by the U.S. Coast Guard?

Yes. The U.S. Coast Guard National Maritime Center lists Health & Safety Institute First Aid/CPR/AED course HSINST-203 as meeting the applicable First Aid and CPR training requirements referenced in 46 CFR 11.201 for merchant mariner credentials, subject to the acceptance conditions.

Is this an STCW course?

No. The Coast Guard acceptance specifically states that HSINST-203 does not satisfy STCW training, standards-of-competence, or practical-assessment requirements.

Do I get both HSI certification and USCG paperwork?

For the USCG-designated class, 910CPR will provide the HSI course completion documentation and the HSINST-203 Recognition of Participation documentation used for the applicable Coast Guard credential pathway.

Where is the class held?

Public training is held at 910CPR, 4018 Shipyard Boulevard in Wilmington, North Carolina, unless a class listing states another location.
