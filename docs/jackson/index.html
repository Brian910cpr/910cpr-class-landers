Friends & Family CPR

Hands-on CPR practice for families, caregivers, and neighbors.

The American Heart Association’s Family & Friends CPR program teaches lifesaving skills including adult Hands-Only CPR, child and infant CPR, AED use, and choking response. It is designed for people who want practical CPR skills for everyday life rather than a job-required course completion card.

Informational + hands-on. Practice is the focus. No certification card.
