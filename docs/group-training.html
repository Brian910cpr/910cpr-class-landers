Common group-training questions

Can training happen at our facility?

Yes, after we confirm the address, room, group size, equipment plan, travel, and instructor schedule.

Does every employee need the same course?

No. Match each employee to the role and written requirement. Clinical staff may need BLS while nonclinical responders may need First Aid, CPR, and AED.

What should our coordinator provide?

Share the exact requirement wording, employee roles, headcount, training address, workday constraints, and preferred dates. That is enough to begin.
