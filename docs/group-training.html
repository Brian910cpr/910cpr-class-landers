Before You Ask

Group training questions

Where does 910CPR provide on-site training?

910CPR is based in Wilmington and serves organizations across southeastern and coastal North Carolina. Tell us your city and preferred timing so we can confirm travel and instructor availability.

How many people can we train?

Group size depends on the course, space, equipment, and instructor ratio. Send an estimated headcount—even if it may change—and we will recommend the practical class setup.

Can part of the course be completed online?

Yes, blended options are available for several programs. Online coursework can reduce time together, but required hands-on skills still must be completed in person.

What equipment or space do we need?

We bring the course training equipment. You provide a suitable room with enough clear floor or table space for the group. We will confirm course-specific needs before the date.

What certification will employees receive?

That depends on the selected program and certifying body. 910CPR supports AHA, ARC, and HSI pathways where appropriate. We will match the card to the requirement you provide rather than guess.

How much does group training cost?

Pricing varies by program, headcount, delivery format, location, and any online course materials. The request form collects the details needed for an accurate quote; published package pricing appears on the next page where available.

How far ahead should we schedule?

Earlier requests provide more choices, especially for large teams or firm compliance deadlines. If your need is urgent, include the deadline and several possible times so we can identify the fastest realistic option.

Can employees take a public class instead?

Yes. If only a few people need certification or your team cannot meet together, use 910CPR’s live public course pages to compare actual dates and book individual seats.
