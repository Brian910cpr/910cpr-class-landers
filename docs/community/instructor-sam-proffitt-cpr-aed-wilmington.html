Local News Coverage: Why Early CPR and AED Access Matter

In a July 2, 2025 WWAY report about CPR and automated external defibrillators, Sam Proffitt was identified as Wrightsville Beach Ocean Rescue Captain and discussed the role AEDs play in emergency response.

Proffitt described AED availability across Wrightsville Beach response vehicles and beach equipment and emphasized that businesses should consider both AED access and CPR-trained staff. The report also highlighted the importance of the first minutes of a cardiac emergency, when bystander CPR and early AED use can begin before professional responders arrive.

Read the original WWAY report: “Doctor, first responder stresses value of AED and CPR training.”

What That Means in a 910CPR Classroom

CPR training is more useful when it connects the classroom skill to the real sequence of an emergency: recognize the problem, start CPR, get an AED moving toward the patient, and continue effective care until additional help arrives.

910CPR provides CPR, AED, first aid, BLS, ACLS and PALS training for individuals and organizations throughout the Wilmington area and southeastern North Carolina.
