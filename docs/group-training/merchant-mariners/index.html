Primary sources

What do the authorities say?

The Coast Guard lists HSI HSINST-203 for specified Merchant Mariner Credential first-aid and CPR requirements. Check your endorsement separately: this listing does not cover STCW endorsements.

Coast Guard course approval listing: HSI HSINST-203; 46 CFR 11.201(i)(1)(ii) and (i)(2)(iii)

Short official excerpt

“satisfies the First Aid training requirements of 46 CFR 11.201(i)(1)(ii) for a merchant mariner credential”

If you have a written requirement, send it with your request. We’ll check the course and format with you before booking.
