Primary sources

What do the authorities say?

OSHA 1917.26 requires a valid first-aid certificate holder at covered terminals while work is in progress. Shipyard and longshoring rules have their own scope.

  • OSHA marine-terminal first aid, 29 CFR 1917.26
  • HSI Regulatory and Approvals Compliance

Check the current rule and your employer or licensing authority’s exact course and format requirement. This page is training guidance, not a legal or licensing determination.

Common question

Does this include water rescue?

A first-aid card does not document lifesaving, water rescue or shipboard watch competence.
