Course type

Primary sources

What do the authorities say?

If medical care isn’t reasonably accessible, the federal construction rule calls for a trained first-aid person at the worksite. Check the rule’s training and documentation language before you choose a class.

Federal construction rule: 29 CFR 1926.50(c)

Short official excerpt

“or equivalent training that can be verified by documentary evidence”

Common question
