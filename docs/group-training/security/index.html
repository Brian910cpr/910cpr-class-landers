Primary sources

What do the authorities say?

OSHA Bloodborne Pathogens obligations turn on occupational exposure. A CPR card does not replace exposure-control duties.

  • OSHA general-industry first aid, 29 CFR 1910.151
  • OSHA Bloodborne Pathogens standard, 29 CFR 1910.1030
  • HSI Regulatory and Approvals Compliance

Check the current rule and your employer or licensing authority’s exact course and format requirement. This page is training guidance, not a legal or licensing determination.
