Course type

  • Fully in person is our usual recommendation. Your team can ask questions, practice together and work through scenarios as a group. It’s often the more economical choice.
  • Blended, where allowed: Employees complete the online coursework on their own schedule, then rotate through the required instructor-led, hands-on skills in pairs or groups of four.

We’re happy to quote either format or both so you can compare, as long as the program, employer and applicable authority allow them.

Primary sources

What do the authorities say?

North Carolina requires CPR instruction for high-school graduation, with hands-on practice. That student requirement doesn’t establish a paid card or a blanket credential for school employees. For staff, check the district’s rules for each role.

North Carolina student requirement; staff guidance: N.C. Gen. Stat. § 115C-83.31(a)(1) and § 115C-81.25(c)(10)

Short official excerpt

“Successful completion of instruction in cardiopulmonary resuscitation as provided in G.S. 115C-81.25(c)(10).”

If you have a written requirement, send it with your request. We’ll check the course and format with you before booking.
