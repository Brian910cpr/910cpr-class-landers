Common questions

Which CPR or certification class should I take?

Start with the exact credential name given by your employer, school, licensing board, or agency. If the requirement names AHA, Red Cross, HSI, BLS, ACLS, PALS, First Aid, or CPR/AED, choose that matching course path.

Does my employer require AHA, or will another CPR provider work?

Requirements vary. Use the provider and course name stated by your employer or licensing body. If the requirement only says CPR, First Aid, or BLS, ask whether AHA, American Red Cross, or HSI is accepted before registering.

Where are 910CPR classes held?

910CPR is based at 4018 Shipyard Boulevard in Wilmington. Available public classes and their locations appear after you choose a course. On-site group training is also available throughout Coastal North Carolina.

Can 910CPR train our workplace or organization on-site?

Yes. We provide on-site CPR, First Aid, AED, and healthcare certification training for workplaces, schools, childcare facilities, healthcare teams, maritime crews, and community organizations.
