How this clears

Electronic payments are reconciled directly from Stripe. For a check, Venmo, Cash App, outside ACH, cash, or another method, use Record Outside Payment. LanderWare stores the date, method, reference, comment and user, then marks the Stripe invoice paid out of band without charging the customer.

Record Outside Payment

This marks the invoice paid in Stripe without charging the customer. The receipt details remain in LanderWare’s audit history.
