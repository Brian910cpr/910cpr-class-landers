Current Guideline Focus

  • Scenario-based work reinforces cardiac arrest, airway and ventilation decisions, rhythm recognition, postarrest care, and team communication.
  • Stroke content is treated as time-sensitive. Recognition and EMS/system activation should be clear and direct.
