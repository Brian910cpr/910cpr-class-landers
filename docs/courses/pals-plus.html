PALS Plus FAQ

Who is PALS Plus for?

It is aimed at experienced pediatric healthcare professionals who want advanced learning beyond the core PALS curriculum.

Is PALS Plus a separate hands-on class?

The advanced material is completed online. The in-person finish is a PALS hands-on skills session, allowing 910CPR to schedule PALS Plus alongside compatible HeartCode PALS skills appointments.

What does $385 include?

Your 910CPR package includes the PALS Plus online learning program and the required hands-on PALS skills session. We sell it as one complete package rather than making you assemble the pieces yourself.

Does the credential identify PALS Plus?

Yes. AHA documentation describes a PALS Plus designation on the PALS Provider eCard for students who successfully complete the PALS Plus requirements.

PALS Plus® and HeartCode® are American Heart Association program names. Course requirements and credentialing remain subject to current AHA rules.
