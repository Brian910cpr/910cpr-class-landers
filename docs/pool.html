The heart behind this event

February 15 means more than a birthday.

Kasia Foltyn, Wilmington's “The Mobile Mermaid,” successfully performed CPR on her father on his 79th birthday, February 15, 2006. He lived another 19 months.

February 15, 2027 would have been his 100th birthday. She chose this day in American Heart Month to honor his life by helping other families learn the lifesaving skills she used.

“Celebrating him and his life in offering this to our community, on his 100th birthday, means the world to me.”
— Kasia Foltyn
Come as you are

Real CPR skills. A welcoming community.

Whether you have never practiced CPR or need a professional BLS course, there's a clear option for you.

What to wear: February may be chilly outside, but the indoor pool area is heated for swimmers and can feel warm. Bring a coat for getting to and from the building, and wear comfortable lighter layers underneath for hands-on CPR practice. You won't need a swimsuit.
