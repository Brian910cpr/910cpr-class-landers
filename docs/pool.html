Why February 15 matters

Kasia Foltyn, known as “The Mobile Mermaid,” chose February 15 in honor of her father. On his 79th birthday, February 15, 2006, she successfully performed CPR on him. He lived another 19 months. February 15, 2027 would have been his 100th birthday.

During American Heart Month, Kasia hopes this community event will help others gain the confidence to respond in an emergency.

“Celebrating him and his life in offering this to our community, on his 100th birthday, means the world to me.” — Kasia Foltyn
