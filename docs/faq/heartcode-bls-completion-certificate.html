How to Download Your HeartCode® BLS Online Completion Certificate

You must complete the survey on the American Heart Association eLearning website before you can download your HeartCode BLS Online completion certificate.

Looking for the hands-on skills check only?
After you finish the online portion, you still need the in-person skills session to complete BLS certification. See HeartCode BLS skills-check availability

There are two ways to reach the survey: you can return to AHA eLearning after you already completed the online course, or you can complete the survey immediately after finishing HeartCode BLS Online.

Option 1: You already completed HeartCode BLS Online

1. Sign in to the AHA eLearning website

Go to elearning.heart.org and select Sign In in the upper-right corner.

2. Open HeartCode BLS and complete the survey

After signing in, go to My Courses. Find HeartCode® BLS Online and select Survey.

3. Download your completion certificate

After you submit the survey, select Download Completion Certificate.

If you need continuing education credit, you can also select Claim CE Certificate. The CE certificate is optional and is not required for your BLS skills session.

Option 2: Download it as you finish the online HeartCode course

1. Select “Exit Exercise”

Once the HeartCode BLS Online course is complete, select Exit Exercise at the top-right of the page.

2. Complete the survey

You will return to the HeartCode BLS course page. Select either Provide Survey button. Both lead to the required survey.

3. Download the completion certificate

After submitting the survey, select Download Completion Certificate.

If you need continuing education credit, you may also select Claim CE Certificate. It is not required for the skills session.

What certificate should I bring to my BLS skills check?

Bring or provide the HeartCode® BLS Online Completion Certificate. The CE certificate is different and is not required for the hands-on skills check.
